# Code of Conduct

WinScope welcomes technical discussion and contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and code;
- provide reproducible technical detail when reporting problems;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information;
- avoid posting sensitive machine reports, credentials, secrets, or personal data;
- respect project safety constraints and responsible security disclosure.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its users.

For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` rather than posting exploit details in a public issue.
